Primary K249 interpretation:
- Find the last `K249 S` record for 3d40000 GPU SMMU. The first negative rc names the internal arm-smmu probe stage producing the current -EBUSY.
- `K249 I grp ok=0` + `K249 I ret rc=-19 s=nogrp` means gmu_user has no IOMMU group and directly explains Phase248 attach -ENODEV.
- `K249 I grp ok=1` + `K249 I ag rc=-19` means the group exists and the -ENODEV is deeper in provider/domain attach.

Do not interpret Phase249 as a fix. It is a diagnostic-only isolation candidate.
