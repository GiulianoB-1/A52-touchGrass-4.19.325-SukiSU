A52 Phase 234 RSCC-focused recorder hardware test

1. Verify SHA256SUMS in the artifact.
2. Flash only package/boot.img to BOOT.
3. Boot once and reproduce the watchdog/reset condition without additional experiments.
4. Collect the complete raw ramoops archive, including all three 256 KiB recorder banks and the frozen 1 MiB image.
5. Decode with the Phase 210 R48 RS48 + CRC32C decoder. The transport format is unchanged. Confirm the identification record contains:

   BOOT rs=ready phase=234 focus=rscc

Interpretation order:

- No RSCCFOCUS record naming sde_rsc:
  inspect platform-driver registration and attach ordering.
- RSCCFOCUS exact-driver match rc=0:
  compatible or platform OF-match mismatch.
- Match succeeds but no RSCCCORE really-probe entry:
  inspect the driver-core attach gate.
- RSCCCORE suppliers rc=-517:
  inspect the recorded supplier links and deferred-probe reason.
- RSCCCORE busprobe enter without RSCC probe enter:
  inspect platform bus probe handoff.
- RSCC probe enter followed by a failing stage:
  use the recorded stage and return code directly.
- RSCC component-add exit rc=0 without RSCC bind enter:
  inspect DRM component-master assembly.
- RSCC bind exit rc=0 followed by no DRMPOST atomic record:
  continue after component binding rather than changing RSC.

Do not use the bundled recorder-v3 decoder. This candidate intentionally retains the Phase 210 R48 RS48 + CRC32C format.
