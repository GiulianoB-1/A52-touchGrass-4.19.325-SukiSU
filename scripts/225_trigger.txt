Phase 225 hardware test

1. Flash only the supplied boot.img to BOOT.
2. Perform one normal boot attempt.
3. Leave the stopped boot for about 180 seconds so SurfaceFlinger retries.
4. Enter recovery and collect the raw 1 MiB RAMOOPS image.
5. Upload A52_RAW_RAMOOPS_*.zip.

Required records:
- GFXPOST 225 ks1
- GFXPOST 225 ks2

Interpretation:
- cs/cr: KGSL core seen/result
- ps/pr: platform probe seen/result
- rs/rr: device registration seen/result
- ns/nr: device_create seen/result
- u: unregister count
- d0: kgsl_driver.devp[0] still present
