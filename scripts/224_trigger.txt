Phase 224 hardware procedure

1. Flash only package/boot.img to BOOT.
2. Attempt one normal boot.
3. After the device stops progressing, restarts, or remains stuck for about
   90 seconds, enter recovery.
4. Collect the same raw 1 MiB RAMOOPS snapshot.
5. Upload A52_RAW_RAMOOPS_*.zip.

Expected markers:
  USRPOST 224 exec
  USRPOST 224 connect-enter / connect-return
  USRPOST 224 ioctl-enter / ioctl-return

Do not wipe data and do not flash any partition other than BOOT.
